# Milestone 1 — Community and candidate distinctions

**Community:** r/personalfinance (public, no login needed to read)

**Device for training (from test.py):** mps (Apple Silicon GPU)

## What I read

A few threads from the top posts of the month, reading the original post and
the top comments. One thread: the poster described their situation and asked
several questions. The top 3–4 comments answered each question in turn with
detailed explanations, and some of them asked the poster to clarify the
situation with another person the poster had mentioned.

## Candidate distinctions

1. **Asking vs. answering.** Some posts describe a personal situation and ask
   for help; others respond to someone else's situation.
2. **Advice with a reason vs. advice without one.** Some replies are short but
   still specific — length isn't the difference. What differs is whether a
   reason comes with it:
   - "Do not pay on the house if it isn't in your name." — what to do, no why.
   - Detailed top comments — what to do, and an explanation of why.
3. **Answering vs. asking for more information.** Some replies don't give
   advice yet; they ask the poster to clarify something first (e.g. the
   situation with another person involved).
